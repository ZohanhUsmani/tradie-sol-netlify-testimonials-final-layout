( 03 ) The playbook · Roofing and solar

Roofing SEO, roofing leads and solar marketing.

Roofing and solar jobs are big tickets, and homeowners research before they call. Roofing marketing that works puts you in front of them at the moment of need, after a storm or a big power bill, and gives them enough proof to pick you.

Our services Websites↗ Local SEO↗ Paid Ads↗ Branding↗

Roofing SEO

Roofing SEO targets the searches that come before big jobs: roof repairs near me, roof restoration cost, gutter replacement and storm damage. We optimise your Google Business Profile, build pages for each service and area, add project galleries with drone photos and explain warranties in plain English. Schema markup helps Google and AI search tools understand your services, and reviews and citations build your rankings month by month.

Roofing and solar websites

A roofing website has to prove quality before anyone climbs a ladder. We build Webflow sites with project galleries, warranty details, finance options, a storm response page and a quote form that asks the right questions, so you get fewer tyre kickers and better leads.

Roofing leads and solar lead generation

Paid ads bring roofing leads fast after storms and through the busy season. For solar, we target searches around panel installation, batteries and rebates, and track every lead to the job it became. You own the leads, so they are never shared with other installers.

Branding for roofers and solar installers

Homeowners trust a roofer or solar installer that looks established. We design brands that look right on a truck, a site sign and a proposal, which helps win the big jobs.

Roofing and solar marketing in Australia and the US

In the US, roofing is one of the most competitive local markets, driven by storms, insurance claims and city pages. In Australia, roof restorations and solar installs are searched by suburb, often alongside rebates. We build each market around the searches and directories its customers use.

How do roofers get more leads?

Rank your Google Business Profile, build service and area pages that show your work, run paid ads through storm season, and follow up every quote quickly. Leads from your own site convert far better than shared leads.

How long does roofing SEO take?

Map rankings usually improve within two to three months. Competitive roofing terms in big US cities can take six to twelve months, while smaller areas move faster.

Do you do marketing for solar installers?

Yes. Solar installers get pages for panels, batteries and rebates, ads on installation searches and a website that explains savings clearly, so homeowners call with realistic expectations.

Should a roofer buy leads or build their own?

Bought leads work short term, but they are often shared with competitors. Building your own through SEO and ads costs more up front and less over time, and every lead is yours alone.

Do you work with roofing companies in the US?

Yes. We set up city pages, US citation sites and Local Services Ads for roofing contractors in the United States, as well as for roofers and solar installers in Australia.
